What is Zivo?

Invoicing made simple

Zivo is a small invoicing tool for freelancers, shop owners and small teams in India. You type in who you are billing and what for, and Zivo turns it into a clean invoice.

You do not need accounting knowledge or any training. If you can fill in a form, you can send an invoice.

Everything you need sits in one place: your business details, your clients, your invoices and whether each one has been paid.

Who makes Zivo

Built in India, for India

Zivo is an independent product made for freelancers and small businesses in India.

Why we built it

Many invoice tools are slow or confusing. We wanted one that anyone can use in a minute.

Your data

Your invoices are private to your account.

25+ designs to match your business

Pick the look you like

Choose from more than 25 invoice designs. Some are plain black and white, some have a coloured header, and some are laid out like a formal tax invoice.

Your logo, business name and details are placed for you. You can move the logo left, centre or right and make it bigger or smaller, and the preview updates as you go.

Switching design never changes your numbers. You can try a few and keep the one you like.

Made for India

GST and rupees

Zivo supports GST on your invoices. Add your GSTIN and tax is shown as CGST and SGST for clients in your state, or IGST for clients in another state. Zivo is a tool, not tax advice, so please check your invoice details with your accountant.

Amounts are in rupees, and you can add your UPI ID or bank details so clients know exactly how to pay.

Who it is for

Freelancers and small businesses

Designers, developers, tutors, photographers, consultants and small shops: anyone who sends a few invoices a week and wants them to look professional.

You can use Zivo free for up to 5 invoices a month. If you need more, Pro removes the limit.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan lets you create 5 invoices every month. Free invoices carry a small Zivo watermark.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time. Pro is prepaid and simply will not renew once you cancel.

Who can see my invoices?

Only you. They are saved to your own account and are not shared with anyone.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Zivo works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer.
