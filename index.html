How it works

1

Add your business

Enter your business name, logo, address and GSTIN once. Zivo remembers them, so every new invoice starts already filled in with your details.

2

Fill in the invoice

Choose or add a client, then list what you are charging for. Zivo works out the subtotal, discount, GST and total as you type, and shows the finished invoice next to the form.

3

Send it and track it

Download the PDF and send it to your client. When the payment arrives, mark the invoice as paid and download a receipt.
